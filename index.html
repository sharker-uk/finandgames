THE IDEA

Build it. Fill it.
Don't ruin it.

More caravans mean more income. More development can mean less charm. The challenge is finding the sweet spot between a beautiful park and a profitable one.

THE STUDIO

Small studio.
Big ideas.

Fin & Games is an independent game studio founded by Sharker.

The studio is focused on management, simulation and indie games — with no particular plan to stay in one genre for too long.

Caravan Park Tycoon is the first project. It's being built from the ground up as a game that is approachable, charming and just deep enough to make you think, “I'll put one more caravan here.”
